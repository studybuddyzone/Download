How to install
  1. Tap “Install”. StudyBuddyPro.apk saves to your Downloads folder.
  2. Allow this source. On the one-time prompt, tap Settings and switch on “Allow from this source” for your browser.
  3. Open the file and tap Install. If Play Protect shows a caution screen, that’s normal for apps installed outside the Play Store.
  4. Open StudyBuddyZone and start studying.
